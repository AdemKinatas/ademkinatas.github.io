Questions

How do I play Hold or Roll?

On your turn, roll the two dice as often as you like. Each roll adds its total to the turn's points, and doubles count twice. A 1 on either die is a bust: the turn's points are lost and the turn ends. Tap Hold to bank the points and pass the turn. Once someone has 100 points (50 in a quick game) and everyone has had the same number of turns, the highest score wins. The ? button in a game shows the rules.

Where can I play it?

Tap Duel on the main screen and choose who to play against: A friend on this phone, The bot (careful, balanced or daring; works offline) or Online. Party is for 3 to 6 players on one phone, and the Daily Dice card on the main screen opens the day's challenge. Duel and Party also offer the Classic game: the higher total takes the round, first to three wins.

What is Daily Dice?

One challenge a day for everyone: 6 turns of Hold or Roll, alone. Every die is rolled on our server and you get one ranked run per day. Afterwards you see your score, your rank among today's players, your day streak, the world top 50 and the countries, ranked by the sum of their five best runs. Yesterday's final boards stay viewable. A new challenge starts every day at midnight UTC. Daily Dice needs an internet connection.

I left my Daily Dice run. What happens?

Your run is kept on the server. Open the Daily Dice card on the main screen and tap Continue my run, also after closing the app. If you end the run or the day ends, the points you banked count and the remaining turns score zero.

How do I play a friend online?

Tap Duel, choose Online and tap Go online. Tap Make a room and send the invite link or the 5-character code; your friend opens the link, or types the code under Join a friend. If one of you loses the connection or switches apps, the app reconnects and the match carries on; a player away for 45 seconds loses the match. No friend around? Play the bot opens a practice room against our bot.

What do other players see of me online?

Only a generated name, a drawn face and your country's flag, plus your score and the reactions you send. You can pick another name or face. Your photos and the names you type never leave your phone, and there is no chat.

How do I delete my online data?

Settings → Online → Delete my online data. It removes your anonymous online player, your country and your Daily Dice runs and streak from our server. If you play online again you start as a new player. Your photos and settings on the phone stay.

How do I roll in Free roll?

Tap Roll, or shake your phone. The dice keep rolling as long as you shake and land when you stop. You can roll 1 to 5 dice at once with the − and + buttons. Doubles, double sixes, three, four or five of a kind and straights get a little confetti celebration. If shaking doesn't work, check Settings → Shake to roll and Shake sensitivity.

How do I put my face on the dice?

Tap the round photo button at the top of the main screen (or Settings → Profile photo), then Take a selfie or From gallery. A close-up with your face centered works best. The photo is cropped to a small square and stays on your phone: it is never uploaded and never shown online. Remove photo deletes it.

How does Pick work?

Choose Pick on the main screen. On the Who? tab, add up to 6 people with a photo and a name; whoever comes up on the die is it. On the What? tab, write up to 6 options or tap one of the Ready-made lists, and the die decides.

What does Premium include?

No ads (no banner and no full-screen ads after matches) and 6 Premium dice styles: Gold, Neon, Marble, Wood, Glass and Candy, also in online play. Premium gives no advantage in Daily Dice or online matches. Classic, Night and Red stay free, and every game mode is free for everyone.

How do I get Premium?

Tap No ads with Premium on the main screen, Premium in Settings, or a locked dice style. Choose Weekly or Monthly (subscriptions that renew automatically until you cancel) or Lifetime (one payment, never renews). The price for your country is shown before you buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Manage subscription on the Premium screen in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge; Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I have Premium but I see ads on my new phone

Open Premium in Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with. This also brings back the Premium dice styles. If a subscription has ended, the ads come back until you renew it.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too; we can't issue them ourselves. iPhone: go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: open your order history in Google Play.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where required by law (for example in the EU and UK) you'll find Settings → Privacy choices in the app. On iPhone you can also switch off tracking in the phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. By default the app uses your phone's language.
