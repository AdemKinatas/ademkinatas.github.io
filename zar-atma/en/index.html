Questions

How do I roll?

Tap Roll, or shake your phone. The dice keep rolling as long as you shake and land when you stop. You can roll 1 to 5 dice at once with the − and + buttons. Doubles, double sixes, three, four or five of a kind and straights get a little confetti celebration.

Shaking doesn't roll the dice

Open Settings and check that Shake to roll is on. If you have to shake too hard, or the dice roll by accident, change Shake sensitivity to High or Low.

How do I turn the sound on or off?

Settings → Sound. It switches the dice rattle, landing and celebration sounds on or off. The light buzz when the dice land has its own switch: Vibration.

How do I put my face on the dice?

Tap the round photo button at the top of the main screen (or Settings → Profile photo), then Take a selfie or From gallery. A close-up with your face centered works best. In a duel or a party game, each player can tap their own photo to set it.

Where is my photo stored?

Only on your phone. The app crops it to a small square and keeps it in its own storage. It is never uploaded. A photo only leaves your phone if you share a result picture yourself. You can remove it any time with Remove photo, and deleting the app deletes it too.

How does a duel work?

Put the phone flat on the table between you. The screen splits in two and the far half is upside down so it faces the other player. Each of you rolls 2 dice; the higher total takes the round. A tie replays the round, and the first to win 3 rounds wins the duel.

How does Party work?

Choose Party at the top of the main screen. Add 3 to 6 players (each can have a name and a photo), pick Rounds to win (3 or 5) and tap Start game. Pass the phone around: everyone throws 2 dice and the highest total takes the round. If there is a tie at the top, only the tied players throw again. The scoreboard shows everyone's face.

How does Pick work?

Choose Pick at the top of the main screen. On the Who? tab, add up to 6 people with a photo and a name; they go on one big die, and whoever comes up is it (who goes first, who pays…). On the What? tab, write up to 6 options, or tap one of the Ready-made lists (what to eat, watch, drink, yes or no), and the die decides. You need at least 2 to roll.

How do I share a result?

After a duel or a party game ends, or after a Pick result, tap Share. The app makes a picture of the result and opens your phone's share menu, so you can send it to any app you like.

What does Premium include?

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes the banner ad and the full-screen ads after duels and party games, and unlocks 6 Premium dice styles: Gold, Neon, Marble, Wood, Glass and Candy. Classic, Night and Red stay free, and every game mode is free for everyone. Choose your style in Settings → Dice style.

How do I get Premium or remove the ads?

Tap Remove ads on the main screen, or Premium in Settings. Pay once and the ads are gone for good and every dice style unlocks.

I bought Premium but I see ads on my new phone

Open Premium in Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. This also brings back the Premium dice styles.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. iPhone: go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: open your order history in Google Play, or see Google Play's refund help page.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where required by law (for example in the EU and UK) you'll find Settings → Privacy choices in the app. On iPhone you can also switch off tracking in the phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. By default the app uses your phone's language.
