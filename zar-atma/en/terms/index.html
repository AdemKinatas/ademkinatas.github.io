1. What the app is

The app is a free dice game and dice roller for entertainment: the "Hold or Roll" game alone against a bot, as a duel on one phone, at a party for three to six players, in the worldwide daily challenge "Daily Dice" and online against a friend or a practice bot; free rolls with one to five dice; the Classic duel and party; a "Pick" die that chooses between friends or options you write; the option to show your own and your friends' photos on the dice on your phone; and a result picture you can share. Results are random and are not meant to be used for betting or games involving real money or prizes; if you do so, it is entirely at your own risk.
