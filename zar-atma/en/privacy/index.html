DiceDice Roller: Face Dice & Duel

Privacy Policy — Dice (Zar)

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Dice app ("the app") on iOS or Android. The app is made by Adem Kınataş ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com.

The short version: we don't have accounts, we don't run our own servers and we don't use analytics. Your photos and settings stay on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and sells an optional "Remove ads" purchase through RevenueCat; those two services process some data, described below.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. We never receive it.

  • Your photos. If you take a selfie or pick a photo for the dice, the app crops it to a small square (about 256 × 256 pixels) and stores that copy locally. The original photo is not kept or modified. In Duel mode each player can have a photo; both are stored the same way. Photos are never uploaded, shared or used for anything other than being shown on the dice.
  • Your settings. Language, dice style, number of dice, vibration, shake sensitivity, and whether the ad-free purchase is active on this device.

You can remove a photo at any time in the app (Settings → Profile photo → Remove photo). Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

2. Permissions

  • Camera — only to take a selfie for the dice, and only when you tap "Take a selfie".
  • Photos / gallery — only to pick a photo for the dice, and only when you tap "From gallery".
  • Motion sensors — to detect when you shake the phone to roll. Motion data is read on the device and not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Tracking (iOS only, App Tracking Transparency) — asks whether Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. The app works the same whether you allow it or not.

You can withdraw any of these permissions in your phone's settings at any time.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you buy "Remove ads", the app shows a banner ad on the free roll screen and a full-screen ad after every second finished duel. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, if you allow tracking; Android advertising ID), and the app set ID on Android;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic and performance data related to ads.

Depending on your choices, Google may use this data to show personalised ads. Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. See:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required): when you first open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related data processing. If you don't agree, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • iOS: after the consent step, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared.

Changing your choices

  • Consent form: in the app, go to Settings → Privacy choices (shown where the consent rules apply). Deleting and reinstalling the app also brings the form back.
  • iOS tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads), where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Stop ads altogether: buy "Remove ads". After the purchase the app no longer loads ads or starts the ads SDK on later launches.

4. In-app purchase (RevenueCat)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to check the purchase and restore it on another device.

  • Payment is processed by Apple or Google. We and RevenueCat never see your card or payment details.
  • RevenueCat receives: an anonymous app user ID it creates (not linked to your name or email), purchase and transaction information (product, price, currency, store, date, receipt/token), and basic device information (operating system, app version, country/storefront, IP address).
  • This is used only to confirm and restore your purchase.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts or a sign-up.
  • We don't run our own servers, so we hold no copy of your data.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting or any other tracking tools of our own.
  • We don't sell your data.

6. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

  • Personalised advertising and access to device identifiers: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw as described above.
  • Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchase validation: performance of the contract you entered by buying "Remove ads" (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Google and RevenueCat may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

7. Retention

Data on your phone stays until you remove it or delete the app. Google and RevenueCat keep data for the periods described in their policies; purchase records may be kept as long as needed to restore your purchase and meet legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Since we don't hold your data ourselves, most requests are best handled directly with Google (https://myadcenter.google.com) or RevenueCat. If you need help, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com and we will do what we can, including asking RevenueCat to delete purchase data linked to your anonymous ID.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected through the ad or purchase services, contact us at ademkinatas@gmail.com and we will help have it removed.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
