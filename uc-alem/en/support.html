Playing

How do I win?

There are two ways: capture the enemy God, or move your own God onto the Tree of Life in the centre of the board. If your God is still standing there when your turn comes round again, you win. Gods cannot capture, so stepping onto the tree is a risk.

A player with no legal move loses. If nobody has won after 40 rounds, the match is a draw.

How do the pieces move?

Tap any piece to see where it can go. The full list, with diagrams, is in the App under Pieces and rules, and How to play walks you through a match step by step.

  • God (Tengri / Zeus): 1 square in any direction; never captures.
  • Hero: up to 2 squares in a straight line, in any direction.
  • Raider (Sky Wolf / Centaur): jumps exactly 2 squares in any direction, over other pieces. It is the only piece that jumps.
  • Seer: up to 2 squares diagonally.
  • Archer: moves 1 square in any direction, or shoots an enemy exactly 2 squares away in a straight line without moving.
  • Soldier: 1 square forward or sideways.
Can I change the difficulty, language, theme or sound?

Difficulty (easy, normal, hard) is chosen on the home screen. Language, day/night theme, sound effects and haptics are in Settings.

Online matches

How do I play with a friend online?

On the home screen choose Play online with a friend. One of you taps Create match and sends the 6-character code; the other enters it under Join with code. Every move is checked on our server.

If you allow notifications, the App tells you when it is your move. An invitation that nobody joins expires after one day.

My opponent stopped playing.

If your opponent hasn’t moved for 15 minutes, a Claim the win button appears in the match.

The App says it couldn’t connect.

Online play needs an internet connection; matches against the AI and on one device work offline. Check your connection and try again. If the problem continues, write to us with the time it happened.

Purchases and ads

When are ads shown?

Never during a match. An interstitial ad appears at most once every 2 finished matches, and never before your first match is over. Rewarded ads are optional and only give cosmetic saga points.

I bought “Ad-free” but I still see ads, or I changed phones.

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If you play as a guest, link an Apple or Google account in Settings first so your purchase follows you to new devices.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested from them:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund help

There are no subscriptions in Üç Âlem; both packs are one-time purchases.

Account and data

I reinstalled the App and my progress is gone.

Guest progress lives on your device. To keep it across reinstalls and devices, link an Apple or Google account in Settings → Account; your statistics are then stored with your account.

How do I appear on the leaderboard?

Link an Apple or Google account and choose a display name in Settings. Only your display name, points and wins are shown.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the App: open Settings → Delete my account and confirm. Your account, statistics, display name, match results and notification token are deleted from our servers immediately.

Without the App: email ademkinatas@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”, from the email address of the Apple or Google account you linked (or tell us that address). We will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.

Guest accounts that were never linked cannot be identified from outside the App; deleting the App removes their data from the device, and you can delete the server copy with the in-app option before uninstalling.

Purchase records kept by Apple and Google are covered by their own policies. Deleting your account does not refund purchases. Details: Privacy Policy.
