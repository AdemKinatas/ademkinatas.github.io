Takama – Rock Paper Scissors

3 seconds. Pick or you burn!

Takama is rock paper scissors turned into a cartoon showdown. Every round has a 3-second countdown: pick in time or lose the round. The winner taunts the loser with a slap, a tongue, a butt dance and more.

  • Play the bot offline, or two players on one phone
  • Online: ranked matches against real players, or a room code for a friend
  • Put your own face on your character
  • Global leaderboard
  • 10 languages, no sign-up
