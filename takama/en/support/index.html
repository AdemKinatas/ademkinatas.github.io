Support

Most answers are below. If something is still wrong, write to us and we'll reply as soon as we can.

How does a round work?

A 3-second countdown starts. Tap rock, paper or scissors before it hits zero; you can change your pick until then. If you don't pick, you burn and lose the round. If both players burn, the round is replayed. Choose 1, 3, 5 or 7 rounds before the match.

How do I play with a friend?

On one phone: choose "2 players"; each player holds one end of the phone. Online: open Online, tap "Create room" and send the 4-letter code to your friend, who enters it under "Join with code". Room matches don't change your points.

How do ranked points work?

Matches against a random online opponent are ranked. You win or lose points depending on your opponent's rating. Leaving a match counts as a loss. The leaderboard shows the top 50 players and your own rank.

Who sees my photo?

Your photo stays on your phone. Only if you switch on "Show my photo to opponents" (Settings › Account & online) is it uploaded so online opponents and the leaderboard can show it. You can switch it off at any time and the uploaded photo is deleted.

Someone's photo is offensive. What can I do?

Tap "Report photo" on the result screen after the match. You won't see that photo again, and a photo reported by several players is removed automatically. For anything urgent, email us with the player's nickname.

Can I change my nickname?

Nicknames are generated so nobody can type an offensive one. Tap "New name" in Settings › Account & online to roll a new one.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

On the old phone: Settings › Account & online › "Link my email", then enter the 6-digit code we email you. On the new phone: Settings › Account & online › "I already have an account, sign in" with the same email. Points, record, streak, language and the ad-free pack come with you.

I bought the ad-free pack but still see ads.

Open Settings and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. The pack is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone) or in the Google Play order history (Android).

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Account & online › Delete my account. Or see the account deletion page for other options.

Online play isn't working.

Check your internet connection and update the app. If nobody is around, you'll be offered a match against the bot. Playing against the bot and on one phone works without internet.
