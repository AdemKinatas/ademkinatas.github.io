Questions

How do I roll?

Tap Roll, or shake your phone. The dice keep rolling as long as you shake and land when you stop. You can roll 1 to 5 dice at once with the − and + buttons.

Shaking doesn't roll the dice

Open Settings and check that Shake to roll is on. If you have to shake too hard, or the dice roll by accident, change Shake sensitivity to High or Low.

How do I put my face on the dice?

Tap the round photo button at the top of the main screen (or Settings → Profile photo), then Take a selfie or From gallery. A close-up with your face centered works best. In a duel, each player can tap their own photo to set it.

Where is my photo stored?

Only on your phone. The app crops it to a small square and keeps it in its own storage. It is never uploaded. You can remove it any time with Remove photo, and deleting the app deletes it too.

How does a duel work?

Put the phone flat on the table between you. The screen splits in two and the far half is upside down so it faces the other player. Each of you rolls 2 dice; the higher total takes the round. A tie replays the round, and the first to win 3 rounds wins the duel.

How do I remove the ads?

Tap Remove ads on the main screen or in Settings. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. After buying, the banner and the full-screen ads are gone for good.

I bought Remove ads but I see ads on my new phone

Open Remove ads and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. iPhone: go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: open your order history in Google Play, or see Google Play's refund help page.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where required by law (for example in the EU and UK) you'll find Settings → Privacy choices in the app. On iPhone you can also switch off tracking in the phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. By default the app uses your phone's language.
