This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Bzzt! mobile game, why it is processed, and what rights you have over that data. The data controller is Adem Kınataş. You can reach us at ademkinatas@gmail.com.

In short

  • You don't need to create an account; we don't ask for your email, phone number or real name.
  • When you play on one device, your statistics, settings and your own words stay only on your device.
  • Voice and video chat only work if you turn them on, and they are not recorded.
  • The free version shows ads. You can change your choice about ad data from within the app.

Data stored on your device

The following data is kept on your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app also deletes this data.

  • Game statistics, achievements and recent games
  • Language, sound, vibration and similar settings
  • Downloaded word packs
  • Your own word lists, unless you use them in a lobby
  • The list of cards you have already seen, used to avoid repeats; if you play online it is sent to the lobby as described below

Data processed when you play online

When you create or join a lobby, the following data is processed on our servers so the game can work:

  • The anonymous player ID generated by the app and the nickname you choose
  • The lobby code, your team and your in-game actions
  • Text messages you send in the lobby and your own word lists that you use in the lobby
  • IP address and technical connection logs, to establish the connection and prevent abuse
  • The list of cards you have seen: sent only while you are in a lobby so the deck avoids repeats, never shown to other players, and deleted with the lobby

Content updates

From time to time the app asks our server whether new or corrected word cards exist, and downloads them to your device if so. This check does not send your player ID, statistics or game data. As with any connection, your IP address reaches the server.

Text, voice and video chat

Your messages are shown only to the players in the lobby. Messages are kept while the lobby is open and are deleted within 24 hours at most after the lobby closes. Reported messages are excluded from this period; see the section below.

Voice and video chat start only when you turn on your microphone or camera, and are transmitted live to the players in the lobby. We do not record or store voice or video. You can turn off microphone and camera permission at any time in your device settings.

Other people in the lobby may record your video or voice by their own means. So only share them with people you trust.

Reports and blocks

When you report a player, the reason for the report, the player IDs and nicknames involved, and the reported messages are recorded. We keep these records for up to 12 months to enforce the rules and prevent abuse. If there is a legal obligation, we may keep them longer. The list of players you have blocked is linked to your anonymous ID.

Ads

In the free version, ads are shown through Google AdMob. Google may process data such as advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interactions.

If required in your region, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. On iPhone and iPad, tracking permission is requested through Apple's system dialog. If you don't give consent, ads may still appear, but they won't be personalized. You can change your choice in the app settings. Owners of the ad-free purchase or Bzzt! Pro don't see ads.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we don't see your card details. We use the RevenueCat service to verify your purchases and restore them to your devices. This service processes the anonymous app ID, the products purchased and transaction information.

Service providers and international transfers

Data is shared only with the providers below, and only to the extent needed for the relevant service to work. These providers' servers may be located outside your country. Transfers rely on the safeguards provided for in the applicable data protection rules.

  • Cloudflare: lobby servers, records and content delivery
  • LiveKit: live voice and video transmission
  • Google AdMob: ads
  • RevenueCat: purchase verification
  • Apple and Google: app distribution and payments

Legal bases

We process data to provide the game service (performance of a contract), to ensure security and prevent abuse (legitimate interests), to meet legal obligations, and on the basis of your consent where consent is required. This statement also includes the information notice under Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) and, to the extent applicable, the GDPR.

Retention periods

  • Lobby data and chat messages: within 24 hours at most after the lobby closes
  • Voice and video: not stored
  • Report records: up to 12 months
  • Purchase records: for as long as legal obligations require

Your rights

Depending on the law of your country, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, object to processing, restrict processing, transfer your data and withdraw your consent. To do so, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com and include the player ID shown in the app settings. Without the anonymous ID, we may not be able to find your data. You also have the right to complain to the competent data protection authority; in Türkiye, this is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

Children

The online features and chat are not intended for children under 13; if your country sets a higher age limit, that limit applies. If you think a child has given us data, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Server connections are encrypted and access to data is restricted. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current text is published on this page, and we announce important changes within the app.
