This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Bzzt! mobile game, why, for how long, and what rights you have. The data controller is Adem Kınataş. You can reach us at ademkinatas@gmail.com.

In short

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Games on one phone, your statistics, settings and your own card lists stay on your phone.
  • Online, you are an anonymous player ID. Lobby data, chat included, is deleted when the lobby closes, at the latest 24 hours after it was created.
  • Lobby chat is text only. The app does not use your microphone, camera, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Consent and tracking are asked only after your first finished game, and Premium players see no ads and are never asked.
  • Settings > Your data > Delete my data deletes your player ID and the blocks you made from our server. What it cannot remove is listed below.

Data stored on your phone

The following stays on your phone and is not sent to us, except where a section below says otherwise. Deleting the app deletes it.

  • Statistics, achievements and recent games
  • An unfinished game, so you can continue it
  • Language, sound, vibration and other settings
  • Your own card lists
  • Downloaded card packs
  • The list of cards you have already seen, so cards do not repeat
  • Your anonymous player ID and its access token, and the names of players you blocked
  • When ads were last shown, so they stay spaced out, and your ad choices

Your anonymous player ID

The first time you play online or download a card pack, our server creates a random player ID with an access token. It is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or your device's advertising identifier. Settings shows the ID, so you can quote it when you write to us.

Data processed when you play online

When you create or join a lobby, our server processes the following so the game can work:

  • Your player ID and the nickname you choose
  • The lobby code, your team and what you do in the game
  • The chat messages you send, and your own card lists if you play them in the lobby
  • The list of cards you have seen, so the lobby's deck avoids them. Other players never see it
  • Your IP address, which reaches the server with every connection. We use it only to limit abuse (too many requests) and do not store it with your player ID

How long lobby data is kept

A lobby closes after 2 hours without activity, after 30 minutes with nobody connected, or 24 hours after it was created, whichever comes first. A practice lobby with bots closes when you leave it. When a lobby closes, all of its data is deleted at once: members, nicknames, chat, game state and seen-card lists. Your messages are shown only to the players in that lobby.

Cards, packs and updates

The app asks our server from time to time whether there are new or corrected cards. That check sends no player ID and no game data. Downloading a Premium card pack sends your player ID's token, so the server can check with RevenueCat that you have Premium. As with any connection, your IP address reaches the server.

Reports

When you report a player, we store: your player ID, the reported player's ID, the lobby code, the reason you chose, the reported chat message (read by the server from the lobby's chat, or, outside a lobby, the text you type) and the time. Nicknames are not stored with a report.

Every new report is emailed to the developer. The email contains the time, the reason, the reported player's ID, the lobby code, the report's number and up to 200 characters of the reported message. It does not contain your player ID. Reports are deleted automatically 12 months after they were filed, unless a legal obligation requires keeping one longer.

Blocks

When you block a player, our server keeps the pair of player IDs so you stop seeing that player's messages, and your phone keeps the blocked player's name so you can unblock them in Settings. A block is kept until you remove it or delete your data. You can block up to 500 players.

Deleting your data

Settings > Your data > Delete my data does the following at once:

  • You leave any lobby you are in
  • Every block you made is deleted from our server
  • Your player ID is replaced with "deleted" on every report you filed
  • A one-way fingerprint (hash) of your player ID is kept, so its old token can never be used again. The ID itself is not kept
  • Your phone forgets the player ID, its token and the names of players you blocked. A new ID is created the next time you play online or download a pack

What deleting does not remove

  • Reports other players filed about you: kept for moderation for up to 12 months
  • Blocks other players made against your old ID
  • A lobby that is still open keeps its copy until it closes, at the latest 24 hours after it was created
  • Your purchases and subscriptions, which belong to your Apple Account. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. RevenueCat keeps its purchase records
  • Statistics, settings and card lists on your phone: delete the app to remove them

Ads

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may process your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics.

Nothing ad-related happens before you finish your first game. Back on the home screen after it, the app shows Google's consent form where your region requires one, then Apple's tracking request. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking and consent to personalized ads; otherwise you may still see ads, but not personalized ones. You can change your choice under Settings > Ad Preferences. With Premium the ad SDK is not started at all: no ads, no consent form, no tracking request.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple handles every payment; we never see your card details. RevenueCat tells the app and our server whether you have Premium. For that it processes the products you bought, your transactions and a purchase ID that is derived one-way from your player token, so other players cannot look up your purchases by your player ID.

Weekly and monthly Premium are subscriptions that Apple renews and charges automatically until you cancel them in your Apple Account settings. Lifetime is charged once.

Service providers and international transfers

Data is shared only with the providers below, and only as far as their service needs. Their servers may be outside your country. Transfers rely on the safeguards that the applicable data protection rules provide for.

  • Cloudflare: game servers, lobby and moderation records, card downloads, and sending report emails
  • Google: AdMob ads and the consent form; the developer's mailbox that receives report emails is on Gmail
  • RevenueCat: checking purchases and subscriptions
  • Apple: app distribution, payments, subscriptions and the tracking request

Legal bases

We process data to provide the game (performance of a contract), to keep it safe and prevent abuse (legitimate interests), to meet legal obligations, and on the basis of your consent where consent is required, as for personalized ads. This policy also serves as the information notice under Türkiye's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) and, where it applies, the GDPR.

Retention periods

  • Lobby data and chat: until the lobby closes, at the latest 24 hours after it was created
  • Reports: 12 months
  • Blocks: until removed, or until the player who made them deletes their data
  • Fingerprint of a deleted player ID: kept, so the old token stays unusable
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat as their own policies and the law require

Your rights

Depending on the law of your country, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. Delete my data in Settings does the deletion at once. For anything else, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com with the player ID shown in Settings; without it we cannot find data linked to you. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Türkiye that is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

Children

Online lobbies and chat are not for children under 13, or under the higher age your country sets. If you believe a child has given us data, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted and access to stored data is restricted. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current text is always on this page with its effective date, and we announce important changes in the app.
