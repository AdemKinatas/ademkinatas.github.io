Having a problem with Bzzt!? Here are answers to the most common questions. If you can't find yours, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com.

How do I play?

Split into two teams. One player describes the word on the card to their team without saying any of the forbidden words below it. A correct guess earns a point; saying a forbidden word costs one. Pass skips a card, up to the pass limit. Before a game you pick the mode, the round time, the passes, the target score, the difficulty and the categories.

When does a game end?

When a team reaches the target score, the round is played out so both teams have had the same number of turns. Then the higher score wins. If both teams are at or over the target with the same score, another round is played.

How do I play online with friends?

  • On the home screen tap Play Online, accept the online rules the first time, then tap Create Lobby.
  • Send the invite with Share Link, or tell your friends the 7-character lobby code.
  • Friends join by tapping the link or typing the code.
  • Once everyone has a team and is ready, the person who created the lobby starts the game.

Can I try online play alone?

Yes. Under Play Online, tap "Try online with bots". A practice lobby opens with three bots that get ready and play their turns by themselves. Nobody else can join it, and it closes when you leave.

The invite link won't open

Make sure the app is installed and up to date. If the link opens a web page instead, type the code shown there into the app. If the lobby has closed, the code no longer works; create a new lobby.

I got disconnected

Open the app again; if the game is still running you return to it. If the player who is describing drops out, the turn pauses for 30 seconds before play moves on.

Continuing an unfinished game

A game on one phone that you leave before the end waits on the home screen: tap Continue, or Discard to throw it away. Statistics and achievements count only games played to the end.

Card packs and Premium

Besides the base deck, there are the Party and Family packs, 150 cards each. Open Packs on the home screen and tap See sample cards to look before you buy. Premium unlocks every pack and removes every ad. It comes as a weekly or monthly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase, all with the same access. Download a pack once and it plays offline.

Restoring a purchase

On the Premium screen (Packs or Settings, then Bzzt! Premium), tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. You need this on a new phone and after Delete my data.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple and renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge: tap Manage subscription on the Premium screen, or open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your data does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Apple takes the payment, so refunds go through Apple under its policies: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

Ads and ad choices

The free version shows a small banner, hidden while a turn runs, and full-screen ads that never interrupt a turn: they come between turns, when you leave a finished game, or when you return to the app after a long time away. Between turns you can also choose to watch an ad for one extra pass. Your ad choices are asked after your first finished game; change them under Settings > Ad Preferences. Premium removes all ads.

Reporting or blocking a player

In a lobby or in the chat, tap the player and choose Block or Report. Every report is emailed to the developer and reviewed. The person who created the lobby can also remove a player and lock the lobby. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact your local authorities.

Deleting my data

Settings > Your data > Delete my data removes your player ID and the blocks you made from our server and takes your ID off reports you filed. Statistics and settings stay on your phone until you delete the app, and purchases stay with your Apple Account. The privacy policy has the details.

Contact us

  • App version (shown at the bottom of Settings)
  • Your player ID (in Settings)
  • When the problem happened and what you were doing
