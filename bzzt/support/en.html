Having a problem with Bzzt!? Here are the answers to the most common questions. If you can't find a solution, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com.

How to play?

Split into teams. The narrator describes the word on the card to their teammates without saying the forbidden words listed below it. A correct guess earns 1 point; saying a forbidden word loses 1 point. The team that reaches the target score wins the game at the end of the round.

How do I play online with friends?

  • On the home screen, tap Play Online and choose Create Lobby.
  • Send the invite with Share Link, or tell your friends the 7-character lobby code.
  • Your friends join by tapping the link or by typing the code in the Join Lobby field.
  • Once everyone has picked a team, the person who created the lobby starts the game.

The invite link won't open

Make sure the app is installed and up to date. If the link doesn't open the app, type the code from the web page that opens into the Join Lobby field in the app. If the lobby has closed, the code won't work; you'll need to create a new lobby.

Voice or video isn't working

  • Make sure the microphone or camera button is turned on in the lobby.
  • Check the microphone and camera permissions for Bzzt! in your device settings.
  • If another app is using the microphone, close it.
  • Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data, and reopening the app.
  • Video chat requires the person who created the lobby to be using Bzzt! Pro.

I got disconnected

Reopen the app; if the game is still going, you'll return to where you left off. If the narrator gets disconnected, the game waits for a short time.

Restoring my purchase

On the Go Premium screen, tap Restore Purchases. You must be signed in with the Apple or Google account you used for the purchase.

Canceling a subscription and refunds

Your Bzzt! Pro subscription and refunds are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; on Android, go to Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Reporting or blocking a player

In the lobby or chat, tap the player's name and choose Mute, Block or Report. The person who created the lobby can also remove the player from the lobby. If there is immediate danger, contact your local authorities.

I want to delete my data

To delete the data on your device, just uninstall the app. For your data on our servers, send the player ID shown in the app settings to ademkinatas@gmail.com.

Contact us

When you write to ademkinatas@gmail.com, we can help faster if you include the following:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Your player ID
  • When did the problem happen, and what were you doing?
