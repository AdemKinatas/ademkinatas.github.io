Help & support

Rock paper scissors with a 3-second countdown. Win the round and your character slaps the other one. Play the phone, a friend on the same phone, or the world online, with your own selfie as the face.

Questions

How does a round work?

A 3-second countdown starts. Tap rock, paper or scissors before it reaches zero; you can change your pick until then. If you don't pick in time you lose the round, and if both players miss, the round is replayed. Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, paper beats rock. The winner of the round makes a cartoon taunt, such as a slap. Before an offline match you choose how many rounds it has.

Where can I play?

On the main screen choose Vs. bot (works offline), 2 players (two people on one phone, each holding one end) or Online. Everything except Online works without an internet connection.

How do I play a friend online?

Tap Online, then Create room, and send the 4-letter code with Share code. Your friend opens Online, taps Join with code and enters it. Friend rooms are never ranked. You can choose the number of rounds for a room.

Why did I get a bot instead of a person?

Random opponent looks for a real player for about 10 seconds. If nobody is looking at that moment, the game gives you a bot so you don't wait. A bot match is labelled with a BOT badge, is not ranked and does not change your points. Practice vs Bot starts such a match right away, with the number of rounds you choose.

How do ranked points work?

A match against a Random opponent (a real person) is ranked, always first to 2 wins. You win or lose points depending on both players' ratings. To keep the leaderboard fair, some results are left unrated, for example repeated matches between the same two accounts within a day, brand-new accounts and results that come only from the other side running out of time. Bot matches and friend rooms never change points, and leaving a match counts as a loss. The leaderboard shows the top 50 players and your own rank.

Who sees my photo?

Nobody but you. Your selfie is shown on your own character on your phone and is never uploaded. Online, your opponent sees a drawn head and your generated nickname, and there is no chat.

How do I put my face on my character?

Tap the photo button on the main screen (or Settings → Profile photo), then Take selfie or Gallery, centre your face in the square and save. Remove photo deletes it.

Can I change my nickname?

Nicknames are generated so nobody can type an offensive one. Go to Settings → Account & online and tap New name for another one.

Can I keep my points on a new phone?

Your points and rank belong to the anonymous account on the phone. There is no email or password, so they can't be moved to another phone, and if you delete the app you may start with a new account. This is the price of having no sign-up.

What does Premium include?

No ads: no banner and no full-screen ads. Premium gives no edge in ranked play and every game mode stays free. Open it with Settings → Premium or the Premium strip on the main screen and choose Weekly or Monthly (subscriptions that renew automatically until you cancel) or Lifetime (one payment, never renews). The price for your country is shown before you buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Manage subscription on the Premium screen in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge; Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your game account does not cancel a subscription.

I have Premium but I see ads on my new phone

Open Settings → Premium and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If a subscription has ended, the ads come back until you renew it.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too; we can't issue them ourselves. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account & online → Delete my account. It removes your guest account, nickname, points and your online match records at once. The page "Delete your account" on this site explains everything that is and isn't deleted.

Online play isn't working

Check your internet connection and update the app. If the connection drops during a match, the app tries to reconnect for a while. Vs. bot and 2 players work without internet. If it still fails, write to us.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK) you'll find Settings → Privacy & ad consent in the app. On iPhone you can also switch off tracking in the phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Premium players are never asked and see no ads. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. By default the app uses your phone's language.
