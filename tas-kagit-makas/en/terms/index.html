1. What the app is

The app is a free rock-paper-scissors game for entertainment: against the phone, two players on one phone, and online (a quick match against a random opponent, a friend room with a code, and practice against a bot). In every round you have a few seconds to pick; the winner of the round makes a cartoon taunt, such as a slap, at the loser's character. You can put your own selfie on your character, on your phone. It is not a gambling service. There are no real-money stakes or prizes in the app, and points, ranks and streaks have no money value.
