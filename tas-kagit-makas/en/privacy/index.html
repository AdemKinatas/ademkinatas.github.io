Privacy Policy — Rock Paper Slap

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Rock Paper Slap app ("the app", also called Taş Kağıt Tokat in Turkish) on iPhone. The app is made by Adem Kınataş ("we", "us"), based in Türkiye. Adem Kınataş is the controller of the personal data described here. If you have any question, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com.

The short version: there is no sign-up, no email address, no password and no chat. The app creates an anonymous guest account with a generated nickname so that online play and the leaderboard work. Your selfie stays on your phone and is never uploaded. The app shows ads from Google AdMob (after your consent, and never with Premium) and sells optional Premium purchases through Apple, with RevenueCat checking them. Each part is described below.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. We never receive it.

  • Your selfie. If you take a selfie or pick a photo for your character, the app crops it to a small square (384 × 384 pixels) and stores that JPEG copy locally. It is shown on your own character in every mode, including online. It is never uploaded, never sent to our servers, never shown to other players and not synced between phones. Online, your opponent sees a drawn head, not your face.
  • Your settings. Language, vibration, sound, your best and current win streak, whether Premium was last active on this device, and counters used to space out ads (how many matches you finished since the last full-screen ad, and when it was shown), and whether you have already been asked about ad consent.
  • Your sign-in key. The app keeps the token of your anonymous guest account (see section 2) in its local storage.

Sharing. When you tap "Share code" for a friend room, the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short message and the room code. What you share goes only to the app or person you choose there.

Device backups. If your phone backs up app data (for example iCloud Backup), this data can be part of that backup. The backup is kept in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

You can remove your selfie at any time in the app (Settings → Profile photo → Remove photo). Deleting the app removes all of this data from the phone.

2. Your guest account and online play (Supabase)

When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous guest account on our backend, which runs on Supabase (Supabase Inc.). This happens even if you only play offline, so that online play is ready when you want it. The account has no email address, no phone number, no password and no name. It is not linked to your Apple Account or to any device identifier.

What the server stores about you:

  • a random account ID, and when the account was created and last used;
  • a generated nickname, stored as three numbers that point into fixed word lists in the app (for example "Grumpy Fox 26"); you can roll a new one, but you can never type a name;
  • your points (rating), wins and losses in ranked online matches;
  • a small copy of your synced settings (language, vibration, best and current win streak), visible only to your account, so they come back if the app signs in to the same account again;
  • for every online match (quick match, friend room or bot practice): the match mode (ranked, friend room, bot), the number of rounds, the account IDs of the players, the score, the state and times of the match, and for every round both players' picks (rock, paper, scissors, or none), the round result and the taunt shown to the winner (chosen at random by the server from a fixed list). While a round is open, your pick is held by the server and nobody, including your opponent, can read it before the round ends. Matches against the bot are stored the same way, with the bot's random pick;
  • for a friend room: the 4-letter room code while the room is open;
  • while you wait for an opponent, a sign that your phone is still there (a "last seen" time, updated every few seconds);
  • wrong room codes you enter: a record of the attempt with the time, kept for one hour, to limit guessing (ten wrong codes in ten minutes pause the entry).

What other players see. Your opponent sees your generated nickname, a drawn head tinted with a colour that depends on your nickname, and your points. The leaderboard shows the top 50 players and your own rank, with nickname, drawn head, points, wins and losses. Only you and your opponents (the players you are paired with) can read your profile (nickname, points, wins and losses) through the game's backend interface. Everyone else sees these only through the leaderboard described above, and nobody else can read when your account was created. Your selfie and your settings are never sent to other players. The app has no chat and nothing you type is sent to other players.

Ranked matches and fair play. To keep the leaderboard fair, the server decides whether a result is rated. It uses the age of the accounts, how many matches an account has finished against how many different opponents, how often the same two accounts have played in the last 24 hours and how many points the winner has gained in the last 24 hours. Matches against the bot and friend rooms never change points.

IP address. Like any online service, Supabase sees your IP address when the app connects. Supabase Auth uses it to limit how many guest accounts one connection can create (ten per hour). We do not copy it into our own game tables. Supabase may keep standard technical logs under its own privacy policy.

How long it is kept:

  • Your account, nickname, points, wins and losses, synced settings and match records stay on the server until you delete your account. An account that has not been used for 365 days (no online match or practice) is deleted automatically, in the same way as when you delete it yourself. For this we keep the date of your last online match or practice.
  • Records of wrong room codes: one hour.
  • Friend rooms nobody joins are cancelled after 15 minutes, quick-match entries from a phone that went silent after 30 seconds, and matches that both players walked away from a couple of minutes after the last round; a cancelled match in which no round was played is deleted one hour after it was cancelled, while the record of a cancelled match with at least one played round stays with the account until it is deleted.
  • Matches against the bot (practice, or a quick match that turned into a bot match): deleted 30 days after they end.
  • A rated result (the two account IDs, the points moved and the time) is kept for 48 hours so the fairness limits above can work, then deleted. It disappears with the winner's account; if the loser deletes their account, it stays for the 48 hours without the loser's ID.
  • Supabase Auth's activity log, which includes the IP address: kept for up to 30 days.

Deleting it. In the app, go to Settings → Account & online → Delete my account. This immediately deletes your guest account, nickname, points, wins and losses, synced settings, and every match record you were part of, including the picks. A match you are in at that moment is ended first (leaving counts as a loss for you). Because a match record belongs to both players, it also disappears from your former opponents' history; the points they earned or lost stay in their totals. After deleting, the app starts a new, empty guest account with a new nickname. The account deletion page of this website explains the same steps. The selfie and settings on your phone are not on our server, so they are removed by the app (Remove photo) or by deleting the app.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: only to take a selfie for your character, and only when you tap "Take selfie".
  • Photos / gallery: only to pick a photo for your character, and only when you tap "Gallery". The app only receives the photo you pick.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): asks whether Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. The app works the same whether you allow it or not.

The app uses no location, contacts, microphone or motion data. You can withdraw any of these permissions in your phone's settings at any time.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have Premium, the app shows a banner ad on the result screen after a match, and a full-screen ad when you leave a result screen, at most once per three finished matches and at least three minutes after the previous one. There are no ads during a round. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). We do not send anything from our servers to Google.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic and performance data related to ads.

Depending on your choices, Google may use this data to show personalised ads. Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. See:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

When you are asked. Not at the first launch. The questions come after you have finished your first match and are back on the main screen, never during a match and never over a result. Later launches start ads right away. If you have Premium, the app never asks and never starts the ads SDK.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required): a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related data processing. If you do not agree, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • iPhone: after the consent step, if it allows ads, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared.

Changing your choices.

  • Consent form: in the app, go to Settings → Privacy & ad consent (shown where the consent rules apply and you do not have Premium). Deleting and reinstalling the app also brings the form back.
  • iPhone tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Stop ads altogether: get Premium. While Premium is active the app loads no ads, shows no consent or tracking prompt and does not start the ads SDK.

5. Purchases and subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)

Premium is sold three ways: a weekly or a monthly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Premium only removes ads; it gives no advantage in matches or on the leaderboard. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to check purchases and subscription status and to restore them on another device.

  • Payment is processed by Apple. We and RevenueCat never see your card or payment details, your name or your email.
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID it creates (not linked to your name, your email or the account ID of our game backend), purchase and subscription information (product, price, currency, store, dates, renewal and expiry status, transaction identifiers and receipt) and basic device information (operating system, app version, storefront country, IP address).
  • This is used only to unlock Premium, keep its status up to date (renewals, expiry, billing problems) and restore it.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6. Crash and usage data

We do not use analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools of our own. If you have allowed it in your iPhone's Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports through App Store Connect. The ads SDK may collect diagnostic data about ads, as described in section 4.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We don't upload your selfie or anything you type, and there is no chat.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't share our backend's data with advertisers.
  • We don't show ads to Premium players or ask them about ad consent or tracking.

8. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

  • Your guest account, online play, the leaderboard and settings sync: performance of the service you ask for when you play online (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in fair rankings and in protecting the service from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)), including the sign-in limits and the ranked-result checks.
  • Personalised advertising and access to device identifiers: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw as described above.
  • Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchase and subscription validation: performance of the contract you entered by buying Premium (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Supabase, Google and RevenueCat may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

9. Retention

Data on your phone stays until you remove it or delete the app. Data on our backend is kept as described in section 2. Google and RevenueCat keep data for the periods described in their policies; purchase records may be kept as long as needed to restore your purchase and meet legal obligations.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK). You can delete your account yourself in the app (Settings → Account & online → Delete my account). Because the guest account is anonymous, we can only find it from the app on your phone; for other requests write to ademkinatas@gmail.com. Requests about ads are best handled directly with Google (https://myadcenter.google.com); we can ask RevenueCat to delete purchase data linked to your anonymous ID.

11. Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It is not in the Kids Category. The app has no chat, no user-generated content and no way for players to contact each other, and it asks for no name, email or phone number. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us at ademkinatas@gmail.com and we will help have it removed.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
