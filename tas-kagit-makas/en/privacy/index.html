Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Who we are

Takama ("the app", "we") is developed by Adem Kınataş, Türkiye. Adem Kınataş is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: ademkinatas@gmail.com.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, who receives it and what you can do about it. It applies to the Takama apps for iPhone and Android and to this website.

The short version

  • You can play without signing up. We create an anonymous guest account so online play and the leaderboard work.
  • Your email address is collected only if you choose to save your account.
  • Your photo stays on your phone unless you switch on "Show my photo to opponents".
  • We show ads through Google AdMob and sell an ad-free pack through Apple and Google.
  • We don't sell your personal data and we don't use it for anything other than running the game.

What we collect and why

  • Account ID: a random identifier created on first launch. Used to run online matches, the leaderboard and syncing. Legal basis: performance of our service to you.
  • Nickname: generated by the app from a word list (for example "Grumpy Fox 26"). Shown to other players.
  • Game data: online match picks and results, points, wins and losses, and your settings (language, vibration, record, streak, hidden photos). Used to run the game and keep your progress.
  • Email address (optional): only if you save your account or sign in on another phone. Used to send one-time sign-in codes. We don't send marketing emails.
  • Photo (optional): only if you switch on "Show my photo to opponents". The image is stored on our servers and shown to your online opponents and on the leaderboard. Anyone who has the image's link can open it. Switching the option off deletes it.
  • Reports: when you report a player's photo we store who reported whom, to remove photos reported by several players.
  • Purchase status: whether you own the ad-free pack. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Technical data: our servers and service providers receive your IP address and device information when the app connects. It is used for security and to deliver the service and is kept only briefly.

Camera and photo library access is used only to pick or take your photo. Photos you don't choose are never read. We don't collect your location, contacts or microphone.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob after matches and as a banner on the result screen. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & ad consent. On iPhone the app does not ask for tracking permission, so the advertising identifier is not used. You can also limit ad personalisation in your phone settings.

Buying the ad-free pack turns off all ads. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Who we share data with

We use these service providers (processors), who handle data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase Inc. – database, accounts and photo storage.
  • Google LLC (AdMob) – ads, as described above.
  • RevenueCat Inc. – checks your purchase status with Apple and Google, linked to your account ID.
  • Apple and Google – app distribution and payments, under their own privacy policies.
  • Supabase Inc. – delivery of sign-in code emails.

Other players see your nickname, points, wins and losses, and your photo only if you share it. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it. We don't sell or rent personal data.

International transfers

Some providers are based in the United States or process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards provided by those companies.

How long we keep data

Account, game data, email and photo: until you delete your account (or the photo, by switching sharing off). Server logs: kept briefly by our providers for security. Purchase records: kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for accounting and tax law.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings › Account & online › Delete my account. This immediately deletes your account, email, nickname, points, match history, synced settings, reports and your uploaded photo. You can also ask us by email; see the account deletion page. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK or California law) you can ask for access to your data, a copy of it, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to its processing, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to ademkinatas@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

Children

Takama is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has given us their email or photo, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted so players can only read what the game needs; opponents' picks stay hidden until the round ends. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it uses data and change the date at the top. Important changes will also be announced in the app.
