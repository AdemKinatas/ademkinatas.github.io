Delete your account — Rock Paper Slap

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Rock Paper Slap has no sign-up. The app creates an anonymous guest account for online play and the leaderboard, and you can delete it yourself at any time, right in the app.

In the app (the fastest way)

  • Open Rock Paper Slap and tap the gear icon (Settings).
  • Tap "Account & online".
  • Tap "Delete my account" at the bottom and confirm.

The deletion happens at once. You need an internet connection.

What is deleted

  • Your guest account, with its nickname, points, wins and losses.
  • The copy of your synced settings (language, vibration, win streak) stored with the account.
  • Every online match record you were part of, with all the picks and results. A match you are in at that moment is ended first, and leaving counts as a loss for you.
  • Records of room codes you entered.

After the deletion, the app starts a new, empty guest account with a new nickname and zero points. Your old points and rank cannot be restored.

What is not deleted

  • Your opponents' own totals: the points they earned or lost against you stay in their points, wins and losses, but the matches themselves are gone from their history too.
  • Purchase records. They are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as the law requires. Premium belongs to your Apple Account and can be restored with "Restore purchases". Deleting your game account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Data that is only on your phone: your selfie and settings. Remove the selfie in Settings → Profile photo → Remove photo, or delete the app to remove everything.

Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Because guest accounts have no email address or name, we can only find your account from details that you give us, such as your nickname, your points and your win and loss record; we may not be able to identify it with certainty, and then we cannot delete it. The in-app deletion is the reliable way. We reply within 30 days. To have purchase data deleted at RevenueCat, write to the same address and we will forward the request.
