Delete your Rock Paper Slap account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Rock Paper Slap and tap the gear icon (Settings).
  2. Tap "Account & online".
  3. Tap "Delete my account" at the bottom and confirm.

Without the app

Email ademkinatas@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address saved in your account, or include your nickname. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Nickname, points, wins, losses and match history
  • Synced settings (language, record, streak)
  • Your uploaded photo and reports you made

What is kept

Purchase records stay with Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required by law. The ad-free pack stays tied to your Apple ID or Google account and can be restored. Points your opponents won or lost against you stay in their totals; the matches themselves are deleted.
