1. What the app is

The app is a free stick-figure fighting game for entertainment, drawn like a notebook doodle: against a bot on your phone, two players on one phone, and online (a quick match against a random opponent, with a bot if nobody is found, a friend room with a code, and practice against a bot). The loser of a fight strikes a silly cartoon "KO pose". Coins and XP are earned by playing and only buy or unlock poses in the app. It is not a gambling service. There are no real-money stakes or prizes in the app, and coins, XP and poses have no money value and cannot be sold, traded or cashed out.

3. Online play and fair play

  • Online play needs an internet connection and an anonymous player that the app creates for you. There is no sign-up. The player is tied to the app on your phone: if you delete the app or switch phones, you start with a new player and the coins and poses of the old one cannot be moved. What is stored, for how long and how to delete it are described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Online you are shown to other players with a generated fighter name. There is no chat, and you cannot type anything that other players see.
  • Play fair. Don't try to manipulate matches or rewards, for example by automating the app, sending requests outside the app, exploiting bugs, creating many players or playing against your own players to collect coins, and don't attack or overload the service. We may cancel matches, remove coins or block access that breaks these rules.
  • What a match pays is decided by the server. Some matches pay less or nothing: for example matches against a bot, repeated matches against the same rival on one day, matches after the daily coin limit, and a match you leave early. Leaving a match counts as a loss.
  • If no human opponent is found within a few seconds, you are matched against a bot. A bot match is clearly labelled.
  • Online play, rewards and poses can change, be reset or be unavailable (for example during maintenance, or when the online service has reached its daily limit and rests until the next day), and a match can end if a player is away for too long. We do not guarantee that online play is available at any time or in your country. Everything except online play works offline.
