Privacy Policy — Doodle Knockout

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Doodle Knockout app ("the app", home-screen name "Doodle KO") on iPhone. The app is made by Adem Kınataş ("we", "us"), based in Türkiye. Adem Kınataş is the controller of the personal data described here. If you have any question, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com.

The short version: there is no sign-up, no account, no email address, no password and no chat. The app creates an anonymous player with a generated fighter name so that online play, coins and poses work. We use no analytics and no crash reporting. The app shows ads from Google AdMob (after your consent, and never the ones you did not ask for with Premium) and sells optional Premium purchases through Apple, with RevenueCat checking them. Each part is described below.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. We never receive it.

  • Your settings. The language you chose, and whether sound effects and music are on.
  • Your anonymous player. The app keeps your random player ID, the signed token that proves it is you to our server, and a random purchase ID (see sections 2 and 5), together with a copy of your profile (fighter name number, coins, XP, wins, losses, poses) so that the app can show it without waiting for the network.
  • Premium status. Whether Premium was active the last time the store answered, so the app knows it before the store answers again.
  • Ad counters. Whether you have already been asked about ad consent, when the app was last used and how many opening ads were shown today. They are used to space out ads (for example, not more than one opening ad a day).

Sharing. When you tap "Send invite" or "Share" the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short message (a room code, or your score). What you share goes only to the app or person you choose there. "Copy code" puts the room code on your clipboard.

Device backups. If your phone backs up app data (for example iCloud Backup), this data can be part of that backup. The backup is kept in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

Deleting the app removes all of this data from the phone.

2. Your anonymous player and online play (our server)

When you first open the app with an internet connection, it creates an anonymous player on our server. This happens even if you only play offline, so that online play is ready when you want it. The player has no email address, no phone number, no password and no name that you typed. It is not linked to your Apple Account or to any device identifier. Our server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.).

What the server stores about you:

  • a random player ID, the day it was created and the last day you were active online;
  • a generated fighter name, stored as one number that points into fixed word lists in the app (for example "Grumpy Fox"); you can roll a new one up to ten times a day, but you can never type a name;
  • your coins, XP, wins and losses, the pose you wear and the poses you bought or unlocked, and small daily counters (coins earned today, free-coin videos used today, name changes today) that keep the economy fair;
  • for each finished online match (quick match or friend room) for two days: the mode, whether it was against a bot, the two player IDs, the score, whether someone left early, the coins paid and whether the reward was doubled, and the time. It is kept so that "Watch an ad, double your coins" works and for support;
  • how many matches the same two players have finished against each other on one day, for two days, so that repeated matches between the same pair stop paying coins;
  • for each rewarded video you watched, for 30 days: the transaction ID that Google reports, your player ID, what it was for (free coins or doubling), whether it was paid, and the time (see section 4);
  • for a friend room: the 5-character room code while the room is open (at most 30 minutes);
  • while a match is running, the state of the match, which belongs to the two player IDs, is kept by the server until the match is over or the room has been idle for 10 minutes, then it is erased. A practice match against a bot and an offline match write nothing about you.

What other players see. Your opponent sees your generated fighter name and the KO pose you wear (a Premium pose is shown only while Premium is active), and sees your moves in the match. The app has no chat; the only messages are fixed taunts chosen from a list. Nothing you type is sent to other players, and nothing about your device or purchases is shown to them.

Fair play. To keep rewards fair, the server decides what a match pays. It looks at who left a match early, how often the same two players have met on one day, how many coins you have earned today and how many free-coin videos you have used today.

IP address. Like any online service, our server receives your IP address whenever the app connects. We use it only at that moment: to limit how many players one connection can create and how often it can call our server, and, through its country, to pick a nearby data centre for a match. We do not save it in our own data. Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs under its own privacy policy.

How long it is kept:

  • Your player, fighter name, coins, XP, wins, losses and poses stay on the server until you delete them. A player that has not been active online for 365 days is deleted automatically, in the same way as when you delete it yourself.
  • Match records and same-pair counts: 2 days. Rewarded-video records: 30 days. Room codes: at most 30 minutes.
  • Cloudflare's own logs: as described in its policy.

Deleting it. In the app, go to Settings → Your data → Delete my data. This immediately deletes your player, fighter name, coins, XP, wins, losses, poses and rewarded-video records from our server. Match records and same-pair counts that mention your ID disappear on their own within two days. After deleting, the app starts a new, empty player with a new fighter name. The delete-account page of this website explains the same steps. The settings on your phone are removed by deleting the app.

3. Permissions

  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): asks whether Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. The app works the same whether you allow it or not.

The app uses no camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts or motion data. You can withdraw the tracking permission in your phone's settings at any time.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have Premium, the app shows banner ads on the menus (home, poses, settings) and on the result screen, and a full-screen ad when you leave a result screen after a match, at most once every 90 seconds. It can also show a full-screen ad when you open the app on its main screen after being away for at least four hours, at most once a day. There are no ads during a fight. You can also choose to watch a rewarded video to get free coins or to double the coins of a match; Premium players can still choose these. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic and performance data related to ads.

Depending on your choices, Google may use this data to show personalised ads. Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. See:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Rewarded videos and your coins. When you watch a rewarded video, the app gives Google your random player ID and a short label (free coins, or double and the match number) with the ad request. When the video is finished, Google calls our server to confirm it, with the transaction ID, the ad unit and these two values; only then do the coins arrive. Nothing else is sent from our server to Google.

When you are asked. Not at the first launch. The questions come after you have finished your first match and are back on the main screen, never during a fight and never over a result. Later launches start ads right away. If you have Premium, the ads that you did not ask for are never shown.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required): a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related data processing. If you do not agree, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • iPhone: after the consent step, if it allows ads, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared.

Changing your choices.

  • Consent form: in the app, go to Settings → Privacy options (shown where the consent rules apply). Deleting and reinstalling the app also brings the form back.
  • iPhone tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Fewer ads: get Premium. While Premium is active the app shows no banners, no full-screen ads and no opening ads; only the rewarded videos you choose to watch remain.

5. Purchases and subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)

Premium is sold three ways: a weekly or a monthly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time Premium Lifetime purchase. Premium removes the ads you did not ask for and unlocks a pack of extra KO poses; it gives no advantage in a fight. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to check purchases and subscription status and to restore them on another device.

  • Payment is processed by Apple. We and RevenueCat never see your card or payment details, your name or your email.
  • RevenueCat receives a purchase ID that our server derives from your player ID with a secret key (so RevenueCat cannot tell which player it is, and it is not your name or email), purchase and subscription information (product, price, currency, store, dates, renewal and expiry status, transaction identifiers and receipt) and basic device information (operating system, app version, storefront country, IP address).
  • Our server asks RevenueCat, with that purchase ID, whether Premium is active when you wear a Premium pose. It keeps the answer in memory for at most a minute and stores no purchase data.
  • This is used only to unlock Premium, keep its status up to date (renewals, expiry, billing problems) and restore it.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6. Crash and usage data

We do not use analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools of our own. If you have allowed it in your iPhone's Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports through App Store Connect. The ads SDK may collect diagnostic data about ads, as described in section 4.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no account to sign in to.
  • We don't have chat, and nothing you type is sent to other players.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't share our server's data with advertisers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash reporting.

8. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

  • Your anonymous player, online play, coins and poses: performance of the service you ask for when you play (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in fair rewards and in protecting the service from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)), including the use of the IP address for rate limits.
  • Personalised advertising and access to device identifiers: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw as described above.
  • Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention, and checking rewarded videos: our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchase and subscription validation: performance of the contract you entered by buying Premium (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Cloudflare, Google and RevenueCat may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

9. Retention

Data on your phone stays until you remove it or delete the app. Data on our server is kept as described in section 2. Google, Cloudflare and RevenueCat keep data for the periods described in their policies; purchase records may be kept as long as needed to restore your purchase and meet legal obligations.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK). You can delete your data yourself in the app (Settings → Your data → Delete my data). Because the player is anonymous, we can only find it from the app on your phone, which shows your player ID in Settings → Your data; for other requests write to ademkinatas@gmail.com and include that ID. Requests about ads are best handled directly with Google (https://myadcenter.google.com); we can ask RevenueCat to delete purchase data linked to your anonymous purchase ID.

11. Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It is not in the Kids Category. The app has no chat, no user-generated content and no way for players to write to each other, and it asks for no name, email or phone number. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us at ademkinatas@gmail.com and we will help have it removed.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
