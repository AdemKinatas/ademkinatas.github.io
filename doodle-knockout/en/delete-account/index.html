Delete your data — Pencil Punch

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Pencil Punch has no sign-up and no account. The app creates an anonymous player for online play, coins and poses, and you can delete it yourself at any time, right in the app.

In the app (the fastest way)

  • Open Pencil Punch and tap Settings.
  • Find the section "Your data".
  • Tap "Delete my data" and confirm.

The deletion happens at once. You need an internet connection.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous player on our server, with its fighter name, coins, XP, wins and losses.
  • The poses you bought or unlocked, and your rewarded-video records.
  • Match records and same-pair counts that mention your player ID are not deleted one by one: they are kept for two days only and then erased automatically.

After the deletion, the app starts a new, empty player with a new fighter name. Your old coins, XP and poses cannot be restored.

What is not deleted

  • Purchase records. They are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as the law requires. Premium belongs to your Apple Account and can be restored with "Restore purchases". Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Data that is only on your phone: your settings. Delete the app to remove everything.
  • Data held by Google for ads and by Cloudflare in its own logs, under their own policies (see the Privacy Policy).

Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, you cannot reach your old player: a reinstalled app starts a new one, and we cannot find an anonymous player without its ID. A player that is not used for 365 days is deleted automatically. If you know your player ID, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and that ID. We reply within 30 days. To have purchase data deleted at RevenueCat, write to the same address and we will forward the request.
