Questions

How does a fight work?

Two stick figures fight a few rounds. Move, jump, punch and kick; walking backwards blocks, and the SUPER button glows when it is full. Whoever wins the match makes the other one strike a silly KO pose. Online quick matches are best of 3; in a friend room, against a bot and on the same phone you choose 1, 3 or 5 rounds.

Where can I play?

On the main screen choose VS BOT (offline, easy, medium or hard), SAME PHONE (two people, one screen), QUICK MATCH (a random opponent online) or WITH A FRIEND (a room with a code). Everything except the online modes works without an internet connection.

How do I play a friend online?

Tap WITH A FRIEND, create a room and send the 5-character code with Send invite (or Copy code). Your friend taps WITH A FRIEND and enters the code to join. The room owner chooses the rounds and starts the match; you can ask for a rematch afterwards.

Why did I get a bot instead of a person?

QUICK MATCH looks for a real player for about 10 seconds. If nobody is looking at that moment, a bot steps in so you don't wait. A bot match carries a BOT tag, pays half the coins and doesn't count as a win. Practice vs bot starts such a match right away and pays nothing.

What do coins and XP do?

In online matches against a person, a win pays 40 coins and 120 XP and a loss 15 coins and 50 XP, up to 600 coins a day. If you play the same rival many times in one day, the extra matches give XP only. Matches against a bot online pay half the coins (up to 200 a day) and never count as wins. Offline modes and practice pay nothing. Coins buy KO poses in the Shop; they can't be bought with money.

How do I get more KO poses?

Two poses are free. Some are bought with coins in the Shop, one unlocks after 25 online wins against people, and the extra poses come with Premium. Open Poses to equip one: it is what your rival sees when you lose.

How do the free-coin videos work?

In the Shop you can watch a short video for free coins (a few times a day), and after a match you can watch one to double your coins. The coins arrive when Google confirms the view to our server, which can take a moment; if they are late, they will show up soon. Premium players can still watch these if they want.

What does Premium include?

No ads you did not ask for (no banner, no ad after a match, no opening ad) and three extra KO poses. Premium gives no edge in a fight and every game mode stays free. Open it with Go Premium on the main screen or in Settings and choose Weekly or Monthly (subscriptions that renew automatically until you cancel) or Lifetime (one payment, never renews). The price for your country is shown before you buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Manage subscription on the Premium screen in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge; Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your data does not cancel a subscription.

I have Premium but I see ads on my new phone

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If a subscription has ended, the ads come back until you renew it.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too; we can't issue them ourselves. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I change my fighter name?

Names are generated so nobody can type an offensive one. In Settings → Your fighter name tap Roll another (up to 10 times a day) and then Use this name.

Can I keep my coins on a new phone?

Your coins, XP and poses belong to the anonymous player on the phone. There is no email or password, so they can't be moved to another phone, and if you delete the app you start with a new player. This is the price of having no sign-up.

Is there chat? Who can see what?

There is no chat. Your rival sees your generated fighter name, the KO pose you wear and your moves in the match; the only messages are fixed taunts from a list.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete my data. It removes your anonymous player, coins, XP and poses from our server at once. The page "Delete your data" on this site explains everything that is and isn't deleted.

Online play isn't working

Check your internet connection and update the app. If you see "Online is resting", the online service has reached its limit for the day and comes back at the time shown; the offline modes still work. If the connection drops during a match, the app tries to reconnect for a while. If it still fails, write to us.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK) you'll find Settings → Privacy options in the app. On iPhone you can also switch off tracking in the phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. By default the app uses your phone's language.
