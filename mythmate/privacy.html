Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use Mythmate (the “App”) on iOS and Android, why it is processed, and what your rights are. Data controller: Adem Kınataş, Türkiye, email: ademkinatas@gmail.com (“we”, “us”).

For users in Türkiye this notice also serves as the information notice required by Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (“KVKK”). For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) also applies.

1. Data we process

Account data

  • If you play as a guest: an anonymous user ID created for your device. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: in addition to the anonymous user ID, the account identifier and email address that the provider shares with us (if you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, Apple’s relay address). We never see your password.
  • Display name: if you choose one in Settings (2–20 characters), it is stored with your account.

Game data

  • Match results: game mode, difficulty, result, number of rounds and points.
  • For online matches, the moves made and the 6-character match code, so the match can run between two devices.
  • If you allow notifications, your device’s push token and app language, so we can tell you when it is your move. Notifications are relayed to Apple/Google through Expo’s push service. You can turn them off in your device settings at any time; the token is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

What other players can see

  • Leaderboard: if you have linked an Apple or Google account, your display name (or “Hero” if you have not set one), your points and your number of wins are shown to other players on the leaderboard. Your email address and account identifiers are never shown. Guests do not appear on the leaderboard.
  • Online matches: your opponent sees the moves you make in that match.

Data stored only on your device

  • Your settings (language, theme, sound, haptics), statistics and an unfinished match are kept on your device so the App works offline. They are removed when you delete the App.

Purchase data

  • The status of your “Ad-free” or “Ad-free + Founder” purchase. Payment is taken by Apple or Google; receipts are validated through RevenueCat. We never see your card or payment details.

Advertising data

  • If you have not bought an ad-free pack, ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA / Android Advertising ID) and ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks) for personalised ads only with your consent.
  • In the EEA and UK, consent is collected through Google’s consent form (UMP). On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown before the advertising identifier can be accessed.
  • If you do not consent, ad personalisation is not used; only non-personalised ads may be shown.

Error reports

  • If the App crashes or hits an unexpected error, a technical report (error trace, device model, operating system and app version) may be sent to the Sentry service. These reports do not include your name, email or advertising identifier and are used only to fix bugs.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. The App has no chat.

3. How ads are shown

  • An interstitial ad appears at most once every 2 finished matches; never during a match and never before your first match has ended.
  • Rewarded ads are optional and only give cosmetic “saga points”.
  • Buying an ad-free pack turns ads off.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers that help us run the App, and only as far as needed:

  • Supabase — hosting of account, match result and online match data (server region: Frankfurt, Germany (European Union)).
  • RevenueCat — purchase receipt validation.
  • Google AdMob — ad delivery (Google’s own privacy policy also applies).
  • Sentry — collecting and analysing error reports.
  • Expo (650 Industries) — relaying online match notifications to Apple’s and Google’s push services.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in, payment processing and notification delivery, under their own terms.

We may also disclose data to competent authorities where required by law.

5. International transfers

The servers of the providers above may be located outside your country. Such transfers rely on the safeguards provided for in KVKK Article 9 and Chapter V of the GDPR (for example, standard contractual clauses) or, where required, on your explicit consent.

6. Retention

We keep account and game data for as long as your account exists. Online match invitations that nobody joins are deleted after one day. When you delete your account, your account and match data on our servers are deleted; records we must keep by law (such as purchase records) are kept only for the required period. Apple and Google also keep purchase records under their own policies.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account inside the App: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, statistics, display name, match results and notification token from our servers.

If you no longer have the App, you can request deletion by writing to ademkinatas@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Apple or Google account; see the support page. Purchases are tied to your Apple / Google account; deleting your account does not refund them.

8. Ad and privacy choices

You can change or withdraw your ad consent at any time under Settings → Ad and privacy preferences (shown where consent is required). On iOS you can also manage tracking permission in the device’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, in the device settings under Privacy → Ads (on some devices Google → Ads).

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

Under KVKK Article 11 you have the right to learn whether your data is processed and request information about it, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties to whom it is transferred, to request rectification, erasure or destruction and notification of these to recipients, to object to a result against you arising from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Send requests to ademkinatas@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or with the data protection authority in your EEA country or the UK.

10. Children

Mythmate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access is restricted by per-user rules at the database level. Online moves are checked by our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will announce material changes in the App or on this page, and the “Last updated” date will change.
