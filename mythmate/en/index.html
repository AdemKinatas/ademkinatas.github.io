Mythmate

A chess-like strategy board game from mythology. Tengri’s Turkic-Mongol pantheon faces Zeus and the Greek gods on a 7×7 board.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon
  • Two ways to winCapture the enemy God, or hold the Tree of Life with your own God for one turn.
  • Six short rulesLearn in a minute; a match takes 3–8 minutes.
  • Play your wayAgainst the AI (easy, normal, hard), two players on one device, or online with a friend using a 6-character code.
  • Drawn in ancestral stylesTurkic pieces carved like rock art in basalt; Greek pieces in black-figure on terracotta.

Fair play

Mythmate is free with ads. Ads never appear during a match. One-time purchases remove ads and add cosmetic items; there are no subscriptions, and purchases give no gameplay advantage.

Available in Türkçe, English, Deutsch, العربية and 日本語.
