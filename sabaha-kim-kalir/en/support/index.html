Who Survives the Night?

Help & support

A secret-roles party game for 4 to 16 players. Vampires strike at night, the village talks and votes by day. Play with friends by code or link, with text and voice chat, or practice alone against bots.

Questions

How does a game work?

Everyone gets a secret role: vampire, villager, doctor or seer. At night the vampires choose a victim, the doctor protects one player and the seer checks whether one player is a vampire. By day everybody talks, then comes the vote: the player with the most votes is out. The village wins when all vampires are out; the vampires win when they are as many as the rest.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Create room, enter your nickname and send the 5-character code or the invite link with Invite. Friends tap Join, enter the code (or open the link) and take a seat. The host sets the timers and the roles and starts the game when 4 to 16 players are in. A room is for the people you invite: there is no matchmaking with strangers.

How do I practice alone?

Tap Practice with bots. You get a table with five bots; the host (you) can add or remove bots (4 to 16 seats) and start. Bots play random legal moves and carry a BOT tag; they never chat or use voice. Nothing about a practice game is recorded. You need an internet connection.

How does voice chat work?

In a room with friends tap Join voice and allow the microphone. Voice is optional and goes directly between the phones; it is never recorded or stored. The game decides who can hear whom (for example, only vampires hear each other at night). Up to about 10 players can be in the voice chat at once. It is not available in practice or when you are the only person in the room.

What does "Hide my IP" do?

In a direct voice connection the other players can see your IP address. With Hide my IP your voice goes through a relay run by Cloudflare, so they see only the relay's address. The relay does not record anything. If the relay's daily allowance is used up, the option rests until the next day and voice may connect directly.

I can't hear anyone, or nobody hears me

Check that the microphone is allowed (iPhone Settings → the app → Microphone), that the silent switch and the volume are not muting the game, and that you tapped Join voice. Voice follows the rules of the game: at night only vampires hear each other, and the dead hear the dead. A weak connection can also cause this; leaving and joining voice again helps.

Why does the app make sounds, and can I turn them off?

The game plays short sounds when night falls, day breaks, the vote opens, someone dies, a game ends, and a tick in the last 10 seconds. Turn them off in Settings → Sound. They are quieter while voice chat runs, and the phone's silent switch mutes them.

What are the chat rules?

Be kind. No insults, harassment, hate speech, sexual or violent content, spam, personal information or offensive nicknames. Messages and nicknames are checked automatically in 18 languages, so some words are masked or refused. The full rules are in the Terms of use.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap a player in the list and choose Report (pick a reason) or Block. A report goes to us with that player's recent messages for review, and nobody is told that you reported. Block hides their chat from you and keeps you out of each other's voice chat; you can undo it in Settings → Blocked players. In voice you can also mute a player for the current room.

Can the host remove someone?

Yes. The host can kick a player and lock the room so that nobody new can join. Lock the room once all your friends are in.

I was banned. What now?

A player who breaks the community rules can be banned from online play; the app then says "This device can no longer play online". If you think it is a mistake, write to us (see Contact) and tell us your player ID (Settings → Your data). Practice with bots is also online, so it is not available while banned.

What does Premium include?

No ads (no banner, no ad after a game, no opening ad). Nothing else changes: every role and mode is free for everybody. Open it from the main screen or from Settings and choose Weekly or Monthly (subscriptions that renew automatically until you cancel) or Lifetime (one payment, never renews). The price for your country is shown before you buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Manage subscription on the Premium screen in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge; Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your data does not cancel a subscription.

I have Premium but I see ads on my new phone

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If a subscription has ended, the ads come back until you renew it.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too; we can't issue them ourselves. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I change my nickname?

Yes. You can type a new nickname when you create or join a room. Names that are offensive are refused, and if two players choose the same name, the second gets a number (for example "Ada 2").

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete my data. It removes your anonymous player and your blocks from our server at once. The page "Delete your data" on this site explains everything that is and isn't deleted.

Online play isn't working

Check your internet connection and update the app. If you see that online is unavailable, the service has reached its limit for the day or is under maintenance and will come back. If the connection drops during a game, the app tries to reconnect and puts you back at your seat. If it still fails, write to us.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK) you'll find Settings → Privacy options in the app. You can also switch off tracking in the phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. By default the app uses your phone's language.
