Privacy Policy — Who Survives the Night? Mafia

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Who Survives the Night? Mafia app ("the app", home-screen name "Who Survives?", in Turkey "Sabaha Kim Kalır?") on iPhone and iPad. The app is made by Adem Kınataş ("we", "us"), based in Türkiye. Adem Kınataş is the controller of the personal data described here. If you have any question, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com.

The short version: there is no sign-up, no account, no email address and no password. The app creates an anonymous player on our server and asks you to type a nickname for the rooms you join. Text chat is filtered and is kept only while the room exists, unless it is reported. Voice chat goes directly between the players' phones and is never recorded or stored; other players in the voice chat can see your IP address unless you turn on "Hide my IP". We use no analytics and no crash reporting. The app shows ads from Google AdMob (after your consent) and sells optional Premium purchases through Apple, with RevenueCat checking them. Each part is described below.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. We never receive it.

  • Your settings. The language you chose, the theme, and whether sound effects are on.
  • Your anonymous player. The app keeps your random player ID and the signed token that proves it is you to our server (see section 2), your nickname, a note that you accepted the Terms and confirmed that you are 13 or older, and the last room code so that a game can be resumed after the app was closed.
  • Your statistics. Games played and won are counted only on your phone.
  • Mute choices. Whom you muted in a voice chat, for the current room.
  • Premium status. Whether Premium was active the last time the store answered, so the app knows it before the store answers again.
  • Ad counters. Whether you have already been asked about ad consent, when the app was last used and how many opening ads were shown today. They are used to space out ads (for example, not more than one opening ad a day).

Sharing. When you tap "Invite" or "Share" the app opens your phone's share sheet with a short message (a room code or link). What you share goes only to the app or person you choose there.

Device backups. If your phone backs up app data (for example iCloud Backup), this data can be part of that backup. The backup is kept in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

Deleting the app removes all of this data from the phone.

2. Your anonymous player and online play (our server)

The first time you use an online feature (create or join a room, or "Practice with bots"), the app shows the Terms, asks you to accept them and to confirm that you are 13 or older, and then creates an anonymous player on our server. The player has no email address, no phone number and no password. It is not linked to your Apple Account or to any device identifier. Our server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.).

What the server stores about you, outside a room:

  • a random player ID, the day it was created and the last day you were active online;
  • the version of the Terms you accepted;
  • a ban flag, if you were banned (see below);
  • a small daily counter of voice-relay requests (see "Voice chat");
  • your blocks: for every player you blocked, that player's random ID and the nickname they had when you blocked them, so that the block works in later rooms and you can see and undo it in Settings.

What the server keeps while a room exists: the room code, the nickname you typed, the players, their roles and what happens in the game, and the text chat (at most the last 200 messages) of all three chat channels (public, vampires and the dead). A room is deleted, with everything in it, 10 minutes after the last person left, or after 24 hours at the latest; the same is true of the room code. A "Practice with bots" room is deleted when you leave it and nothing about it is recorded.

What other players see. Everybody in the room sees your nickname. They see what you write in the public chat, and, if you are a vampire or dead, your messages in those channels to the players who may read them. Nothing else about you, such as your player ID, is shown to other players. The server never sends a player's role to someone who may not know it.

Chat filter. Our server checks nicknames and chat messages against word lists in 18 languages. A message may be masked or refused. This check is automatic and keeps nothing.

Reports, blocks and bans. If you report a player (from the player list or the end screen), our server stores a report: your nickname and your player ID as the reporter, the reported player's nickname and ID, the room code, the reason you chose, and the last 20 messages written by the reported player in that room, including messages in channels that you could not read (such as the vampire channel), so that we can judge the report. A report is not visible to any player and is sent to us by email (the report, nickname, reason and message excerpt). A report is kept for 12 months and then deleted. We may ban the reported player: a ban is a flag on their player, and the app then shows "This device can no longer play online". Blocking a player hides their chat from you and keeps you out of each other's voice chat; the blocked player is not told.

Voice chat. Voice is optional: it starts only when you tap "Join voice" and allow the microphone. It works peer to peer (WebRTC): your phone sends your voice directly to the other players in the voice chat. Our server only passes the connection messages between you. We never receive, record or store your voice, and there is no voice recording on any server.

  • Your IP address. To connect directly, the players in the voice chat exchange network information, so they can see each other's IP address (and thereby your approximate location). If you do not want that, turn on "Hide my IP": your voice is then sent through a relay (TURN) run by Cloudflare Realtime, so other players only see the relay's address. The relay passes the voice on without recording it. To make this possible, our server asks Cloudflare for temporary relay credentials (valid for about 30 minutes) when you join voice. If the daily relay allowance of the service is used up, "Hide my IP" is paused until the next day and voice may connect directly again.
  • Who hears whom. The game rules decide who may hear whom (for example, vampires talk to each other at night). You can mute any player, and block them to keep them out of your voice chat.
  • Voice is not moderated by us in real time because we do not listen to it. Use mute, block and report.

IP address. Like any online service, our server receives your IP address whenever the app connects. We use it only at that moment, to limit how many players one connection can create and how often it can call our server. We do not save it in our own data. Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs under its own privacy policy.

How long it is kept:

  • Your player, terms version and blocks stay on the server until you delete them. A player that has not been active online for 365 days is deleted automatically, in the same way as when you delete it yourself.
  • Rooms and everything in them (nicknames, roles, chat): deleted 10 minutes after the last person left, 24 hours at the latest.
  • Reports (including the message excerpt): 12 months.
  • Cloudflare's own logs: as described in its policy.

Deleting it. In the app, go to Settings → Your data → Delete my data. This immediately deletes your player, your blocks and the room codes you created from our server. Reports about you or made by you stay for up to 12 months, because they protect other players; they are not linked to anything else about you. A player ID is the only thing that links data on the server to your phone; after deleting, the app starts a new, empty player. The delete-account page of this website explains the same steps. The data on your phone is removed by deleting the app.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: only for voice chat, after you tap "Join voice". The app asks for it the first time. You can turn it off any time in your phone's Settings; the rest of the app works without it.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): asks whether Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. The app works the same whether you allow it or not.

The app uses no camera, photos, location, contacts or motion data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have Premium, the app shows banner ads on the home screen, in the lobby and on the end screen, and a full-screen ad when you leave an end screen, at most once every 90 seconds. It can also show a full-screen ad when you open the app after being away for at least four hours, at most once a day. There are no ads during a game (night, day or vote). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic and performance data related to ads.

Depending on your choices, Google may use this data to show personalised ads. Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. See:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

When you are asked. Not at the first launch. The questions come after you have finished your first game and left its end screen, never during a game. If you have Premium, no ads are shown.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required): a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related data processing. If you do not agree, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • iPhone and iPad: after the consent step, if it allows ads, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared.

Changing your choices.

  • Consent form: in the app, go to Settings → Privacy options (shown where the consent rules apply). Deleting and reinstalling the app also brings the form back.
  • Tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • No ads at all: get Premium. While Premium is active the app shows no banners, no full-screen ads and no opening ads.

5. Purchases and subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)

Premium is sold three ways: a weekly or a monthly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time Premium Lifetime purchase. Premium removes the ads; it changes nothing else in the game. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to check purchases and subscription status and to restore them on another device.

  • Payment is processed by Apple. We and RevenueCat never see your card or payment details, your name or your email.
  • RevenueCat creates its own random anonymous ID for your app installation (it is not your player ID, your name or your email, and our server does not know it) and receives purchase and subscription information (product, price, currency, store, dates, renewal and expiry status, transaction identifiers and receipt) and basic device information (operating system, app version, storefront country, IP address).
  • Our server does not receive purchase information and stores none.
  • This is used only to unlock Premium, keep its status up to date (renewals, expiry, billing problems) and restore it.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6. Crash and usage data

We do not use analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools of our own. If you have allowed it in your phone's Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports through App Store Connect. The ads SDK may collect diagnostic data about ads, as described in section 4.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number, and there is no account to sign in to.
  • We never record or store voice chat.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't share our server's data with advertisers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash reporting.

8. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

  • Your anonymous player, rooms, chat and voice chat: performance of the service you ask for when you play (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its players from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)), including the chat filter, reports, blocks, bans and the use of the IP address for rate limits.
  • Personalised advertising and access to device identifiers: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw as described above.
  • Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchase and subscription validation: performance of the contract you entered by buying Premium (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Cloudflare, Google and RevenueCat may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

9. Retention

Data on your phone stays until you remove it or delete the app. Data on our server is kept as described in section 2. Google, Cloudflare and RevenueCat keep data for the periods described in their policies; purchase records may be kept as long as needed to restore your purchase and meet legal obligations.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK). You can delete your data yourself in the app (Settings → Your data → Delete my data). Because the player is anonymous, we can only find it from the app on your phone, which shows your player ID in Settings → Your data; for other requests write to ademkinatas@gmail.com and include that ID. Requests about ads are best handled directly with Google (https://myadcenter.google.com); RevenueCat's data about your purchases can be handled through RevenueCat or Apple.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and asks you to confirm that you are 13 or older before you play online. It is not in the Kids Category. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us at ademkinatas@gmail.com and we will help have it removed.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
