1. What the app is

The app is a free social-deduction party game for entertainment, for 4 to 16 players, in the style of "Mafia" and "Werewolf". Secret roles (vampires, villagers, a doctor and a seer) are dealt; the vampires strike at night and everyone talks and votes by day. You can play with friends in a room that you create or join with a code or a link, with text chat and optional voice chat, or play "Practice with bots" alone. There is no matchmaking with strangers: you meet only the people you invite. It is not a gambling service. There are no real-money stakes or prizes in the app.

5. Moderation: filter, report, block, kick and bans

  • Filter. Nicknames and chat messages are checked automatically against word lists in 18 languages. A message can be masked or refused. The filter is not perfect.
  • Report. You can report a player from the player list or the end screen. We receive the report by email with the reported player's recent messages from that room (all chat channels) and review it. We may act on it, but we are not obliged to reply to each report.
  • Block. You can block a player: you will not see their chat and you will not be in a voice chat together. The blocked player is not told. You can undo a block in Settings.
  • Mute. You can mute any player in voice chat for the current room.
  • Host controls. The host of a room can remove (kick) a player and lock the room.
  • Bans. If you break these rules we may ban your player. A banned player can no longer play online; the app tells you so and shows how to contact us. We may also remove content or end a room. We do not owe you a warning for serious violations. If you think a ban is a mistake, write to us.
  • Voice chat is not recorded and we do not listen to it, so we cannot check what was said in it: please mute and block anyone who bothers you, and report them. Players in a voice chat can see each other's IP address unless one of them uses "Hide my IP" (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Please don't use the app to talk about real people in a harmful way. Other players are real people.
