Delete your data — Who Survives the Night? Mafia

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Who Survives the Night? Mafia has no sign-up and no account. The app creates an anonymous player for online play, and you can delete it yourself at any time, right in the app.

In the app (the fastest way)

  • Open the app and tap Settings.
  • Find the section "Your data".
  • Tap "Delete my data" and confirm.

The deletion happens at once. You need an internet connection.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous player on our server, with the version of the Terms you accepted and the ban flag, if any.
  • Your blocks, and the room codes you created.
  • On your phone, the app forgets your player, your nickname, your statistics and your acceptance of the Terms.
  • Rooms are not deleted one by one: they, and the chat in them, are erased automatically 10 minutes after the last person left (24 hours at the latest).

After the deletion, the app starts a new, empty player the next time you play online.

What is not deleted

  • Reports about you or made by you. They are kept for 12 months and then erased automatically, because they protect other players. They contain the nickname, the reason and the message excerpt, and are not linked to anything else about you.
  • Purchase records. They are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as the law requires. Premium belongs to your Apple Account and can be restored with "Restore purchases". Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your phone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Data that is only on your phone: your settings. Delete the app to remove everything.
  • Data held by Google for ads and by Cloudflare in its own logs, under their own policies (see the Privacy Policy).

Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, you cannot reach your old player: a reinstalled app starts a new one, and we cannot find an anonymous player without its ID. A player that is not used for 365 days is deleted automatically. If you know your player ID, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and that ID. We reply within 30 days. To have purchase data deleted at RevenueCat, write to the same address and we will forward the request.
