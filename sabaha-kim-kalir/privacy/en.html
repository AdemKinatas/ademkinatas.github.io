Privacy Policy

Last updated: 08.11.2026

Who we are

Who Survives the Night? ("the game") is developed and operated by Adem Kınataş, an independent developer in Türkiye. Contact: ademkinatas@gmail.com.

This policy is written with Türkiye’s data protection law (KVKK) and the EU GDPR in mind.

What we process

As a guest: the nickname you type and the messages you send during a game. These live only in the server’s memory while the room is open and are never written to a database.

As a member: your email address, nickname, password (we store only a one-way hash of it), whether you own the ad-free pack, and the results of your games (room code, role, team, win or loss, survived or not).

Voice chat: when you join voice chat, your voice travels directly between the players in your room (peer to peer, WebRTC), encrypted. If a direct connection can’t be made, it passes through a relay server (Cloudflare) that cannot decrypt it. Voice is never recorded or stored on any server. To set up the connection, your IP address is visible to the other players’ devices in voice chat and to Google’s connection (STUN) servers. If you turn on "Hide my IP address" in the voice panel, your voice always goes through the relay server and other players can’t see your IP address.

Reports: when you report a player, we keep the room code, both nicknames (and account ids for members), the reason and that player’s latest messages in the room so a moderator can review them.

Error reports: if the game hits an error on your device, the error message, where in the code it happened, the page you were on, the device type (web, iOS, Android) and the game version are sent to our server’s logs so we can fix it. Your name, account and IP address are not included; these logs are kept for at most 30 days.

Security: your IP address is kept in the server’s memory while you are connected, to prevent abuse, and is not logged. When you sign up or sign in, Supabase keeps your IP address and browser details in its own security logs to protect your account.

Purchases: payment happens through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see card details. RevenueCat processes your account id and purchase record so the purchase can be linked to your account.

Ads: in the app, ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device’s advertising id and IP address to show, measure and personalise ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first; without it you see non-personalised ads. On iOS, if you don’t allow Apple’s tracking permission, the advertising id is not shared. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the "Ad privacy settings" link at the bottom of the home screen. You can reset the advertising id or turn off personalised ads in your device settings. The web version shows no ads. Members with the ad-free pack see no ads.

What we keep on your device

Your theme, language and session are kept in the browser’s local storage or the app’s device storage. The list of players you blocked is kept only for the browser session. We use no tracking or advertising cookies. These are needed for the game to work.

Why, and on what legal basis

To provide the game and your account (contract), to keep it safe and prevent abuse (legitimate interest), to personalise ads (consent) and to meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your data. We share it only with the providers needed to run the service: the host of the game server, Supabase (accounts and database, in Frankfurt, EU), Cloudflare (voice relay), RevenueCat (purchase validation), Google (ads in the app), the email service that sends account emails, and Apple / Google (app stores).

Some of them are located outside Türkiye (in the EU and the US). These transfers are needed to provide the service and rely on these providers’ data processing agreements.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Sign in in the app, or in the game’s web version in a browser, and tap “Delete my account” to delete it permanently; your game results and ad-free pack go with it. If you can’t reach the app, write to ademkinatas@gmail.com from your account’s email address and we will delete it within 30 days.

Reports are kept for at most 12 months.

Error reports are kept for at most 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to processing and withdraw your consent. Write to ademkinatas@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Authority or the data protection authority where you live.

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13. We delete accounts we learn belong to a child under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version here and update its date.
